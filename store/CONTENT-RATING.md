# Content rating and target audience prep

Complete the official IARC questionnaire inside Play Console. Use the answers below as prep, not as a rating result. Google and IARC assign the final rating.

## Game content facts

- Violence: Fantasy action combat against non-human enemies and bosses. The player uses fire and other abilities. There is no realistic gore or graphic injury focus.
- Blood: No realistic blood content is a design feature of the game.
- Fear or horror: Some realms and bosses use dark, void, chaos, and blood moon themes. There are no jump-scare horror mechanics.
- Language: No profanity is intentionally included in game copy.
- Sexual content: None.
- Drugs, alcohol, or tobacco: None.
- Gambling: The game does not offer real-money gambling or cash prizes. It has optional in-app purchases for virtual items and a monthly crate that grants stated virtual currency rewards.
- User interaction: Players can create a username and appear on public leaderboards. There is no chat, voice chat, friend messaging, photo sharing, or public free-form posting.
- Personal information sharing: A player's username and leaderboard statistics are visible to other players.
- Location sharing: The game does not ask players to share their location with other players.
- Digital purchases: Yes. Gem packs, temporary boosts, Remove Ads, and one auto-renewing subscription.
- Ads: Yes. Optional rewarded ads and capped interstitial ads served by AdMob.

## Target audience recommendation

Select 13 and older as the target audience for the first release.

Reasons:

- The game permits account creation and public display names.
- It contains ads and in-app purchases.
- It is not designed as a Families or Designed for Families release.
- The privacy policy states that the game is not directed to children under 13.

Do not add child-directed content, child-focused characters, or Families program claims without a separate policy and ads review.
