# Phoenix Fall Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 25, 2026
Developer: Phoenix Websites AI
Contact: hello@phoenixwebsites.ai

Phoenix Fall is a mobile game. This policy explains what information the game and its backend collect, why it is used, and how players can control it.

## Information we collect

### Account information

Creating an account is optional. If you create one, we collect and store:

- Email address
- Username or display name
- A password hash for email accounts. We do not store your plain text password.
- Google account identifier and email address if you choose Google sign-in

Your username, level, experience, trophies, and battle results may appear on public leaderboards.

### Gameplay and device-saved information

We store gameplay information needed to run and sync the game, including:

- Level, experience, coins, gems, unlocked worlds, upgrades, abilities, trophies, and unlocked enemies
- Battle scores and best results
- Purchase entitlements, subscription status, Remove Ads status, revive tokens, boost status, and monthly crate status
- Settings and progress stored locally on your device, including guest progress

### Purchases

Google Play processes payments. We do not receive or store your full payment card details. We receive purchase information needed to verify a transaction and grant the purchased item, including the product ID, purchase token or order identifier, purchase state, and subscription expiry when applicable.

### Advertising

The Android app uses Google AdMob. Google may collect and use device and advertising identifiers, IP address, approximate location derived from IP address, ad interactions, and diagnostic information to provide, limit, measure, and improve ads, as described in Google's privacy disclosures. Rewarded ads are optional. Interstitial ads are shown only under the frequency and placement rules described in the game documentation.

### Support communications

If you email us, we receive your email address and the content of your message.

## How we use information

We use information to:

- Create and secure accounts
- Save and sync progress
- Operate campaign, battle, codex, trophy, and leaderboard features
- Verify purchases and provide purchased content
- Provide rewarded ad grants and prevent duplicate reward claims
- Prevent cheating, fraud, and abuse
- Respond to support and deletion requests
- Maintain and improve the game

We do not sell personal information.

## Sharing

We share information only with service providers needed to operate the game, including:

- Google Play for payments, subscriptions, and purchase verification
- Google AdMob for advertising
- Google sign-in services if you choose that sign-in method
- MongoDB Atlas and Render for backend database and hosting services

These providers process information under their own terms and privacy policies. Your public leaderboard information is visible to other players.

## Retention and deletion

Account and gameplay data are kept while your account exists, unless a longer period is required for security, fraud prevention, accounting, or legal reasons.

You can delete your account in the game from the profile screen. Deleting the account removes the user account and its purchase entitlement records from the live game database. Local guest data can be removed by clearing the app storage or uninstalling the app. You may also request deletion by emailing hello@phoenixwebsites.ai from the account email address. See the account deletion page for the full process.

Purchase records held by Google Play are controlled by Google and are not deleted from Google's systems when you delete your game account.

## Security

We use encrypted connections for backend communication and store passwords as bcrypt hashes. No system is perfectly secure, but access to production systems is restricted to the developer.

## Children

Phoenix Fall is not directed to children under 13. Do not create an account if you are under 13. If you believe a child under 13 created an account, contact hello@phoenixwebsites.ai so the account can be reviewed and deleted.

## Changes

We may update this policy when the game's data practices change. The updated policy will be posted at the same public URL with a new effective date. Material changes will be reflected in the Play Console Data safety form when required.
