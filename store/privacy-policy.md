Privacy Policy

Phoenix: Rise of the Flame
Last updated: October 24, 2026
Published by Phoenix Websites AI - contact: hello@phoenixwebsites.ai

This policy explains what the Phoenix: Rise of the Flame game collects, why, and your choices. Plain language on purpose.

What we collect

Account data (only if you create an account): your email address, a display name you choose, and whether you signed in with Google. Passwords are stored only as salted one-way hashes. We never see or store your Google password.

Game data: your progress and activity in the game, including level, experience, virtual currency balances, moves and skins you own, world scores and completion, battle results, trophies, settings, and purchase entitlements (which items you own, never your payment details). Signed-in players store this on our servers so it syncs across devices and appears on leaderboards. Guest players store this only on their own device.

Technical data: basic server logs (time, request, error details) needed to run and secure the service.

What we do NOT collect: your real name, postal address, phone number, contacts, photos, files, precise location, or advertising ID for our own tracking. The game has no chat and no user-generated content beyond display names and scores.

Ads: the game shows ads provided by Google AdMob. If you buy Remove Ads, interstitial ads stop. Rewarded ads play only when you tap to watch one (for a revive or a boost). AdMob may collect device and ad-interaction data under Google's policies: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

Purchases: all purchases are processed by Google Play Billing. We receive confirmation of what was bought so we can grant it in game. We never receive your card or bank details.

Leaderboards: display names and scores are shown publicly inside the game. That is the point of a leaderboard. Nothing else about you is shown.

Sharing: we do not sell personal data. Data is shared only with the processors that run the game (MongoDB Atlas for the database, our hosting provider, Google AdMob, and Google Play), and if required by law.

Retention and deletion: account data is kept while your account exists. You can delete your account and all associated cloud data instantly in the game under Profile - Delete Account. Guest data lives on your device and is removed by Settings - Reset Local Progress or by uninstalling.

Children: the game is not directed at children under 13, and accounts should not be created by children under 13.

Security: traffic is encrypted in transit (HTTPS), passwords are hashed with bcrypt, and access to the database is restricted.

Changes: if this policy changes in a material way, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new date.
