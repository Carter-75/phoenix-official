## Full description

Take flight as the Phoenix in a fast action game built around movement, timing, and powerful abilities.

Battle through 10 realms, from the Ember Wastes and Cerulean Depths to the Blood Moon and Abyssal Rift. Fight through each run, face a realm boss, earn coins and gems, and unlock stronger builds for the next attempt.

FEATURES

- 10 campaign realms, each with its own boss identity and theme
- Campaign mode and Battle mode
- Unlockable and upgradeable abilities, including Fire Breath, Drill Attack, Burst, Phoenix Turret, Rebirth, and Aura
- Permanent progression upgrades for health, speed, magnetism, damage, attack speed, range, aura, and homing
- Trophies, an enemy and realm codex, and a Classic Move Archive with 75 catalogued moves
- Optional free account with a unique display name, cloud sync, and campaign and battle leaderboards
- Guest play with progress saved on the device
- Optional rewarded ads for a revive, a temporary 2x coins and XP boost, a free upgrade, or another in-game reward
- Optional purchases for gems, temporary multipliers, Remove Ads, and the Monthly Crate Pass

Phoenix Fall is free to play. Ads and purchases are optional. Interstitial ads are limited to natural breaks after a run and are never shown to Remove Ads owners or subscribers.

Account creation is optional. If you create an account, your display name and leaderboard results may be visible to other players. You can delete your account from the profile screen.
