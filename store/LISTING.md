Full description:

Rise from the ashes and take flight.

Phoenix: Rise of the Flame is a fast, fiery flying shooter. You are the phoenix: weave through waves of enemies across 10 worlds, unlock and equip 75 moves, and bring down 10 named bosses, from the Inferno Guardian of the Ember Plains to the Eternal Phoenix itself.

DRAG TO FLY, TAP TO UNLEASH
Drag anywhere to steer. Double tap for a piercing strike, triple tap for a devastating burst, and hold to channel a beam of pure flame. Every move can also be fired from its own button, so the fight never slows down.

10 WORLDS, 10 BOSSES
Ember Plains, Shadow Realm, Crystal Caverns, Storm Peaks, Void Nexus, Phoenix Sanctum, Frozen Wastes, Dragon Throne, Chaos Dimension, and the Eternal Flame. Each world brings new enemy mixes and a boss with its own attack patterns.

75 MOVES TO COLLECT
Phoenix Inferno, Dragon's Breath, Solar Flare, Void Spike, Time Pierce, Phoenix Omega, and dozens more across three move types. Earn coins in battle, build your perfect loadout, one move per slot.

BATTLE MODE
Face an AI rival phoenix in a 1 vs 1 duel. Climb the battle leaderboard with every win.

ACCOUNTS AND LEADERBOARDS
Play instantly as a guest, or create an account with email or Google to save progress to the cloud and compete on four leaderboards: Global, Campaign, per-world, and Battle. Your guest progress carries over when you sign up.

SKINS AND TROPHIES
Unlock Ember, Frost, Eclipse, Solar, and Verdant phoenix skins, earn trophies, and fill your codex with every foe you defeat.

FAIR AND FREE
The game is free. Ads appear only between runs, never mid-fight, and watching an ad is always your choice when it can revive your phoenix or grant a 2x boost. Remove Ads is available as a one-time purchase, and every purchase runs through Google Play.
