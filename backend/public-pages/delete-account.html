Delete your Phoenix Fall account

Developer: Phoenix Websites AI
Game: Phoenix Fall (com.phoenix.fall)
Contact: hello@phoenixwebsites.ai

Delete inside the app

  1. Open Phoenix Fall.
  2. Sign in to the account you want to delete.
  3. Open your profile.
  4. Choose Delete Account and confirm.

This is the fastest method. The game deletes the account and its entitlement records from the live game database.

Request deletion by email

If you cannot sign in, email hello@phoenixwebsites.ai from the email address used for the account.

Subject: Phoenix Fall account deletion

Include only:

  • The account email address
  • The in-game username, if known
  • A statement that you want the Phoenix Fall account deleted

Do not send your password, Google password, payment card details, or government ID.

We may ask you to confirm control of the account email address before deleting the account. Confirmed requests are processed without unnecessary delay, with a target of 30 days or less.

What is deleted

Deletion removes the live account record and its associated game data, including:

  • Email address and username
  • Password hash or Google account link
  • Synced progress, currency balances, upgrades, trophies, and leaderboard entry
  • Purchase entitlement and ad reward records stored by the game backend

What may remain outside the game database

  • Google Play may retain purchase and subscription records under Google's policies.
  • Email messages you sent to support may be retained for support and legal records.
  • Guest progress stored only on your device is removed by clearing Phoenix Fall app storage or uninstalling the app.

Deleting an account does not cancel an active Google Play subscription. Cancel the Monthly Crate Pass in Google Play first if you do not want it to renew.

See also the Phoenix Fall Privacy Policy.
