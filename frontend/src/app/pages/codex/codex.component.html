How to Play

Hold the button to ascend. Avoid or destroy enemies to stay alive. Defeating enemies drops Coins.

Defeating a Boss will yield precious Gems. Use your currencies in the Shop to unlock powerful upgrades!

Died? You can rise again: watch an ad to revive (up to twice per run), spend gems, or use a Revive Token earned from rewarded ads.
